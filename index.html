📖 Learn Ayurveda

Ancient Kerala wisdom, explained simply

Ayurveda is one of the world's oldest holistic healing systems, developed in India over 5,000 years ago. The word comes from Sanskrit: Ayur (life) + Veda (knowledge).

It is based on the belief that health and wellness depend on a balance between mind, body, and spirit. Unlike modern medicine which focuses on treating disease, Ayurveda focuses on preventing it.

🌍 Ayurveda is recognized by the World Health Organization (WHO) as a traditional system of medicine.

Ayurveda identifies three biological energies called Doshas that govern our body and mind:

  • Vata (Air + Space): Controls movement, breathing, circulation. When imbalanced: anxiety, dry skin, constipation.
  • Pitta (Fire + Water): Controls digestion, metabolism, intelligence. When imbalanced: anger, inflammation, acidity.
  • Kapha (Earth + Water): Controls structure, immunity, stability. When imbalanced: weight gain, congestion, laziness.
Everyone has a unique mix of all three doshas — knowing yours helps maintain balance through diet, herbs, and lifestyle.

Modern science has confirmed many Ayurvedic claims through research:

  • Turmeric (Curcumin): Proven anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties in hundreds of studies.
  • Tulsi: Shown to reduce cortisol (stress hormone) and support immune function.
  • Ginger: Clinically proven to reduce nausea, especially motion sickness.
  • Neem: Proven antibacterial and antifungal — used in modern toothpastes and skincare.
💡 Ayurveda complements — but does not replace — modern medicine. Always see a doctor for serious conditions.

Kerala is globally recognized as the heartland of authentic Ayurvedic practice. The state's tropical climate, heavy rainfall, and rich biodiversity make it ideal for growing medicinal plants.

  • Kerala has over 900+ Ayurvedic hospitals and thousands of traditional practitioners (Vaidyas).
  • The Ashtangahridayam, a major Ayurvedic text, was written in Kerala by Vagbhata.
  • Kerala's Panchakarma treatments attract visitors from around the world.
🏡 Many Kerala households still use home remedies — turmeric milk, tulsi tea, ginger for digestion — passed down through generations.

Herbal remedies work well for minor, everyday ailments. Knowing when to use them — and when not to — is essential.

  • ✅ Use herbs for: Minor cuts, mild fever, rashes, nausea, indigestion, cough/cold
  • ❌ See a doctor for: Deep wounds, high fever (above 102°F), severe allergic reaction, chest pain, difficulty breathing, symptoms lasting more than 3 days
⚕️ AyurAid is a first aid guide. It does not replace professional medical advice.

Every herb in your AyurAid kit contains specific active compounds that create healing effects:

  • Turmeric → Curcumin: Blocks inflammatory enzymes, kills bacteria
  • Ginger → Gingerols & Shogaols: Stimulate digestion, reduce nausea signals to brain
  • Tulsi → Eugenol: Antibacterial, reduces fever, calms the nervous system
  • Aloe → Aloin & Acemannan: Soothe burns, moisturize skin, reduce inflammation
  • Neem → Nimbin: Powerful antifungal and antibacterial agent

Used For
✂️ Cuts & Wounds 🔥 Inflammation 🦠 Infection 💛 Skin glow
How to Use
Apply a paste of turmeric powder with 2–3 drops of coconut oil directly on cuts or wounds. It is antiseptic and helps stop minor bleeding.
  1. Take 1 teaspoon of turmeric powder
  2. Add 2–3 drops of coconut oil or plain water
  3. Mix into a thick paste
  4. Apply gently over the wound or affected area
  5. Cover loosely with a clean cloth if needed
⚠️ Stains skin and cloth. Do not apply on deep wounds. See a doctor if bleeding doesn't stop.
Used For
🧴 Skin Infections 🌡️ Rashes 😤 Pimples / Acne 🦷 Oral health
How to Use
Boil neem leaves and use the cooled water to wash infected skin, pimples, or minor wounds. Strong antibacterial and antifungal properties.
  1. Take 10–12 fresh neem leaves
  2. Boil in 1 cup of water for 5 minutes
  3. Let it cool to room temperature
  4. Strain and use the water to wash the affected skin area
  5. Pat dry gently. Repeat twice daily
⚠️ For external use only in first aid. Bitter taste — not for internal use without guidance.
Used For
🤒 Mild Fever 😷 Cough & Cold 😤 Sore Throat 🧠 Stress relief
How to Use
Boil 8–10 tulsi leaves with ginger in a cup of water, strain and sip warm. Eases headache, mild fever, cough and cold.
  1. Take 8–10 fresh tulsi (holy basil) leaves
  2. Add a small piece of ginger (half inch)
  3. Boil both in 1 cup of water for 5–7 minutes
  4. Strain, add a tiny pinch of black pepper if available
  5. Sip warm — up to 2 cups per day
⚠️ Avoid during pregnancy. Consult a doctor before surgery.
Used For
🔥 Minor Burns ☀️ Sunburn 🌡️ Skin Rash 💆 Soothing
How to Use
Slit a fresh aloe vera leaf and apply the cool gel directly on the rash or burn. Soothes irritation and speeds healing.
  1. Choose a thick, mature aloe vera leaf
  2. Cut a 3–4 inch piece from the outer leaf
  3. Slit it open lengthwise to expose the gel
  4. Scoop out the clear gel with a spoon
  5. Apply directly to the burn, rash, or irritated skin
⚠️ Patch-test first if you have sensitive skin. Use only the clear gel — avoid the yellow latex layer.
Used For
🤢 Nausea 🍽️ Indigestion 💨 Gas / Bloating 🚗 Motion sickness
How to Use
Chew a small piece of fresh ginger with a pinch of rock salt, or sip ginger tea. Relieves nausea, gas and indigestion.
  1. Cut a 1-inch piece of fresh ginger
  2. Chew directly with a pinch of rock salt for quick relief, OR
  3. Boil sliced ginger in 1 cup of water for 5 minutes
  4. Strain, add honey or lemon if preferred
  5. Sip warm slowly
⚠️ Avoid large doses if on blood thinners or before surgery.
Used For
💇 Hair Loss 👁️ Eye Health 🩸 Blood Sugar 🤢 Mild Diarrhoea
How to Use
Chew a few fresh curry leaves in the morning, or boil with water and sip. Helps with mild diarrhoea and nausea. Rich in antioxidants.
  1. Take 10–15 fresh curry leaves
  2. Wash them well under clean water
  3. Chew directly on an empty stomach in the morning, OR
  4. Boil in 1 cup of water for 5 minutes
  5. Strain and sip warm
⚠️ Use fresh leaves only. Dried leaves have reduced potency.
